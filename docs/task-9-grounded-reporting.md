# Task 9: Evidence-Grounded Report Wording, Explanations, and Recommended Actions

## 1. Overview & Objective

Task 9 redesigns report wording, forensic explanations, positive claims, and recommended actions across AsliOffer. Building directly upon Task 8's separation of warning strength, investigation coverage, and offer authenticity, Task 9 ensures that:

1. **Every summary is grounded in the structured assessment**: The system reports what supported adverse signals, review concerns, and coverage gaps exist, rather than relying on generic boilerplate.
2. **Unsupported claims of legitimacy or safety are completely eliminated**: Clean searches, company domain alignment, and an absence of complaints never prove an individual offer is genuine, authentic, or safe.
3. **Reasons and coverage gaps are unified across VerdictReasoner and ReportGenerator**: A shared presentation layer prevents divergence, preserves signal codes, and retains concrete coverage gaps across all outcomes.
4. **Positive claims (green flags) reflect only completed, supported checks**: Local document scanning is separated from external scam complaints; salary plausibility is stated only when benchmarking completed cleanly; domain alignment is not exaggerated into mailbox control or hiring authority.
5. **Official company information is strictly derived from supported CompanyAgent results**: Document-provided links are never promoted to official channels, and conflicting findings are handled conservatively and deterministically.
6. **Recommended actions are signal-specific, proportionate, and actionable**: Actions directly address the detected risks (advance fees, credential demands, domain mismatches, agency authorization, salary questions, or provider outages) without instructing users to overreact to unverified evidence or inventing non-existent verification portals.
7. **Every report explicitly notes that the specific offer remains unauthenticated**: The employer has not verified issuance of the document.

## 2. Outcome Wording Rules

Each outcome summary is generated by [`derive_summary()`](../backend/app/services/report/presentation_helper.py) in `app.services.report.presentation_helper`:

### HIGH_RISK
- **Adverse Signal Naming**: Explicitly names the supported critical warning signal (e.g. advance-fee demand, equipment deposit, net-banking credential solicitation).
- **Proportionate Warning**: Describes a serious warning ("such demands are characteristic of employment fraud") without asserting that legal fraud has been conclusively proven.
- **Coverage Gap Preservation**: If external searches experienced provider outages or coverage gaps, notes that external checks were incomplete while emphasizing that the detected local adverse signal warrants immediate caution.
- **Authenticity Statement**: Concludes with: *"The employer has not authenticated this individual offer; public web checks cannot verify offer issuance."*

### NEEDS_REVIEW
- **Specific Review Concerns**: Explicitly names the detected contextual review concern (e.g. personal webmail domain `@gmail.com` used for corporate hiring, sender domain mismatch, unconfirmed agency mandate, salary outlier).
- **Removal of "Established Company" Boilerplate**: Completely removes the unconditional assertion that the employer is an "established company". Distinguishes cases where public web presence was verified from cases where company identity remains unconfirmed or unavailable.
- **No Invented Problems**: Does not mention recruiter or salary issues unless those specific checks yielded review concerns.
- **Coverage Gap Preservation**: Retains visibility of unresolved checks.
- **Authenticity Statement**: Concludes with: *"The employer has not authenticated this individual offer; public web checks cannot verify offer issuance."*

### CANNOT_VERIFY
- **Specific Gap Explanation**: Distinguishes three distinct inconclusive states:
  1. *Provider Outage*: External search services were unavailable due to provider timeouts or rate limits.
  2. *Completed Search with No Match*: External public searches executed successfully but returned no matching records for the claimed employer or recruiter.
  3. *Missing Essential Inputs*: Critical contact information (e.g. recruiter email or phone) was absent from the document.
- **Non-Accusatory Wording**: Explicitly states: *"Inconclusive evidence is not proof of fraud, but means the offer cannot be substantiated through public channels."*
- **Authenticity Statement**: Concludes with: *"The employer has not authenticated this individual offer; independent verification is required before proceeding."*

### NO_STRONG_RISK_SIGNALS (Legacy VERIFIED)
- **Accurate Scope**: States that completed checks found no supported adverse signals or active scam indicators across investigated public records.
- **Strictly Grounded Positive Statements**: Mentions positive findings *only* when the corresponding check completed and supported them:
  - Employer web presence matched (if `COMPANY_IDENTITY_CHECK` supported).
  - Recruiter domain aligned with corporate domain (if `RECRUITER_AFFILIATION_CHECK` supported).
  - Compensation aligned with market baseline (only if `COMPENSATION_BENCHMARK` completed and supported; omitted if benchmark was not applicable or unavailable).
- **Prohibited Words**: Never describes the offer letter as "verified", "authentic", "genuine", or "safe".
- **Authenticity Statement**: Prominently warns: *"Note: The employer has not authenticated this individual offer; public web checks cannot verify offer issuance. Public web consistency does not prove that this document was legitimately issued."*

## 3. Positive Claims (Green Flags) Requirements

In [`derive_green_flags()`](../backend/app/services/report/presentation_helper.py), positive flags are generated strictly when a check ran to completion and yielded supported/clean results:

| Check | Completed & Clean Condition | Green Flag Wording |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `LOCAL_DOCUMENT_SCAN` | `execution_status == COMPLETED` and `resolution_status == NO_MATCH` | *"Document text scan found no upfront fee demands, deposit requests, or credential solicitation."* |
| `EXTERNAL_SCAM_REPORTS` | `execution_status == COMPLETED` and `resolution_status == NO_MATCH` | *"No matching scam or complaint reports found in external public search."* |
| `COMPANY_IDENTITY_CHECK` | `execution_status == COMPLETED` and `resolution_status == SUPPORTED` | *"Claimed employer public web presence matched; registration status not independently checked."* |
| `RECRUITER_AFFILIATION_CHECK` | `execution_status == COMPLETED` and `resolution_status == SUPPORTED` | *"Recruiter email domain aligns with claimed corporate domain; does not prove mailbox control or hiring authority."* |
| `COMPENSATION_BENCHMARK` | `execution_status == COMPLETED` and `resolution_status == SUPPORTED` | *"Offered compensation package falls within expected market baseline for role."* |

### Critical Guardrails:
1. **Local Scan vs. External Search**: Local document scanning is decoupled from external scam complaint searches. A successful local scan is presented even if SerpApi or external providers suffer an outage.
2. **Empty Search ≠ Clean Recruitment Records**: An empty complaint search is described as "no matching scam or complaint reports found in external public search," never as "clean recruitment records."
3. **Domain Alignment ≠ Mailbox Control**: Aligning email domains with corporate MX/web records does not prove control of the sender mailbox or hiring authority.
4. **Salary Applicability**: If compensation benchmarking was `NOT_APPLICABLE` (e.g. stipend, missing salary) or `UNAVAILABLE`, no salary green flag is emitted.
5. **No Claims for Unavailable Checks**: Checks with status `UNAVAILABLE`, `NOT_CHECKED`, or `UNCONFIRMED` never generate green flags.

## 4. Reason and Coverage-Gap Presentation

Both [`VerdictReasoner`](../backend/app/services/risk/verdict_reasoner.py) and [`ReportGenerator`](../backend/app/services/report/report_generator.py) delegate to [`derive_reasons_and_details()`](../backend/app/services/report/presentation_helper.py):

### Canonical Reason Derivation
- Caller-supplied stale `reason_details` cannot introduce unsupported conclusions; `ReportGenerator` re-derives canonical reasons directly from the structured assessment.
- All supported signal codes are preserved (`ADVANCE_FEE_DETECTED`, `CREDENTIAL_THEFT_DETECTED`, `UNLOCK_PAYMENT_DETECTED`, `CANDIDATE_PAYMENT_DETECTED`, `ADVERSE_PHONE_REPORT`, `ADVERSE_EMAIL_REPORT`, `PERSONAL_EMAIL_DOMAIN`, `SALARY_OUTLIER`, `RECRUITER_DOMAIN_MISMATCH`, etc.).

### Coverage Gap Visibility Across All Outcomes
- Even when `HIGH_RISK` or `NEEDS_REVIEW` takes precedence, concrete coverage gaps remain visible:
  - If employer check failed due to provider outage: `COMPANY_SEARCH_UNAVAILABLE`.
  - If employer check had zero results: `COMPANY_NOT_VERIFIED`.
  - If recruiter contact check failed or was missing: `RECRUITER_NOT_VERIFIED`.
- In `CANNOT_VERIFY`, replaces misleading "unique sources" phrasing with evidence observation wording:
  `"Public evidence is insufficient ({evidence_count} evidence observation(s))."`

## 5. Official Company Information Limitations

In [`derive_official_company_info()`](../backend/app/services/report/presentation_helper.py):
- **Source Restriction**: `website` and `careers_url` are populated *only* from verified, supported `CompanyAgent` findings (`verdict == "VERIFIED"` and `provider_status == "SUCCESS"`).
- **Document Links Excluded**: Unverified links, emails, and URLs provided inside the submitted document are never promoted into `official_company_info`.
- **Conservative Conflict Resolution**: If multiple `CompanyAgent` findings conflict in verdict or present conflicting domains, neither domain is promoted (`website = None`, `careers_url = None`), ensuring deterministic, order-invariant results.
- **MCA Status**: Remains `"Not independently checked"`.
- **Recruitment Policy**: The hardcoded claim (*"Legitimate enterprise HR teams never demand fees..."*) is removed from company-specific facts and set to `"Not independently checked"`. General fee safety guidance is placed in `recommended_actions` instead.

## 6. Signal-Specific Recommended Actions

In [`derive_recommended_actions()`](../backend/app/services/report/presentation_helper.py), actions are selected and deduplicated based on actual findings:

1. **Advance-Fee / Equipment Deposit Demands**:
   - *"DO NOT pay any registration fee, security deposit, laptop charge, or document processing fee under any circumstance."*
   - *"Verify payment policies directly with the employer's official HR department; legitimate employers in India do not charge hiring fees."*
2. **Credential Theft (Passwords / OTPs)**:
   - *"DO NOT disclose banking passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, or net banking credentials; legitimate employers never require account credentials."*
3. **Adverse Contact Reports**:
   - *"Cease communication through the flagged recruiter contact number or email address, as it appears in public complaint reports."*
4. **Suspicious / Unconfirmed Payment Request (UPI / Wallet)**:
   - *"Do not transfer money via UPI, payment links, or mobile wallets for onboarding or training expenses."*
5. **Conditional Cybercrime Reporting Guidance (High Risk Only)**:
   - *"If you suspect employment fraud or have experienced financial loss or credential theft, report the incident to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at https://cybercrime.gov.in or call helpline 1930."*
6. **Free Webmail / Domain Mismatch**:
   - *"The recruiter contacted you from a personal or non-matching email domain. Request written communication from the employer's official corporate email domain."*
   - *"Independently confirm the recruiter's hiring authority by calling the employer's official corporate switchboard or published HR directory."*
7. **Staffing Agency Mandate Uncertainty**:
   - *"Confirm the staffing agency's authorization and candidate representation mandate directly with the client employer's HR department."*
8. **Compensation / Salary Outliers**:
   - *"Request written clarification from the employer regarding total compensation, currency, payment frequency, and employment terms."*
9. **Provider Outages**:
   - *"External search checks were unavailable due to service outages; re-run verification or check official records independently."*
10. **Missing Employer Footprint**:
    - *"Independently look up the employer using official public business registries (such as MCA India at mca.gov.in) or verified business directories."*
    - *"Do not rely on website links, phone numbers, or addresses provided solely within the submitted document."*
11. **Authenticity Confirmation (All Non-High-Risk Offers)**:
    - *"Contact the employer directly through their official published careers portal or HR department to confirm that this specific offer was issued to you."*
    - *"Review standard employment terms, scope of work, and contract conditions carefully before signing."*
    - *"Never share sensitive banking passwords, OTPs, or pay any onboarding charges."*

### Prohibited Guidance (Strictly Avoided):
- Never instructs users to automatically block or report someone solely because evidence is missing, a salary is unusual, or a free email domain is used.
- Never implies that checking LinkedIn alone authenticates a recruiter or offer.
- Never invents ATS verification portals or reference code lookup tools.
- Never asks users to disclose sensitive personal documents or banking details.
